# Sources

## Hebrew Bible
- **Masoretic Text**: Westminster Leningrad Codex (public domain), via tanach.us or Sefaria.
- **Septuagint (LXX)**: Rahlfs-Hanhart; noted wherever the Greek differs from the Hebrew in a way that matters (Isaiah 7:14, Psalm 22:17).
- **Dead Sea Scrolls**: 1QIsa-a (Great Isaiah Scroll), 5/6HevPs (Psalm 22), 4Q174 Florilegium, 4Q175 Testimonia, 4Q521 Messianic Apocalypse.

## Aramaic
- **Targum Onkelos** (Torah) and **Targum Jonathan** (Prophets): Sefaria, CAL (Comprehensive Aramaic Lexicon).
- **Targum Pseudo-Jonathan** and **Neofiti** for Genesis 3:15 and 49:10.

## Rabbinic
- **Babylonian Talmud**: Sanhedrin 93b-99a (the Messiah chapters), Sukkah 52a, Pesachim 54a, Nedarim 32b. William Davidson edition on Sefaria (CC-BY-NC).
- **Midrash**: Pesikta Rabbati 34-37, Midrash Tehillim, Genesis Rabbah.
- **Commentators**: Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Radak, Ramban, Metzudat David, via Sefaria.

## New Testament
- **Greek**: SBLGNT (CC-BY) or NA28 wording.

## English
English renderings in the catalogue are our own, kept close to the Hebrew/Greek word order, and are not copied from a copyrighted translation. Check against JPS 1917 (public domain) and the ESV/NRSV when in doubt.

## Citation rules
- Give the Masoretic verse number first, Christian number in brackets.
- Quote original-language text exactly as it appears in the named edition.
- Every `jewish_reading` should name at least one source (Targum, tractate and folio, or commentator).
